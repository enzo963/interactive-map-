# Sulaymaniyah governorate discovery — 3 November 2026

This batch adds 101 Sulaymaniyah records, which brings Sulaymaniyah from 4 records to 105. 24 of them have map pins.

- Every new record has `status: needs-review` and at least one source.
- `build_sulaymaniyah_records.py` defines the batch, which has two parts:
  - **37 hand-written records** for documented monuments and excavated sites.
  - **64 survey records** generated from the C1-01 candidate file. Each keeps its candidate's source, locator and boundary note.

## Boundaries

- **Halabja.** It became a separate governorate in 2014. Bakr Awa and Gurga Chiya, beside Halabja town, are left out, even though Wikidata still files them under Sulaymaniyah. The Shahrizor survey sites keep the C1 note asking for a check against the Halabja boundary. Tell Begum, near Said Sadiq, carries the same note.
- **Raparin and Garmiyan.** Their sites are kept under Sulaymaniyah, as in the approved Qalatga Darband record.

## Review flags

**Survey records are thin.**

- The 64 generated records give only the survey, the locality and the source locator.
- Their period and present survival are not established.
- They have no coordinates, so they appear in Explore but not on the map.

**Same site, different names.** These pairs need checking before approval:

- Gird-i Shakar / Shakar Tepe
- The Tell Haji and Quchika Tepe records from the Kokushikan figures, against the Shahrizor Survey Project list
- The Hazar Merd caves (written record) and the Tanjaro survey's site TJ 28 "Hazar Merd", which was left out of the survey records to avoid a duplicate
- Tell Kunara, which absorbs the survey name Qadar Kunara

**Approximate or doubtful dating:**

- Srochik castle: its coordinates are rounded and its age is one expert's opinion.
- Sherwana castle: sources give the 18th or the 19th century.
- Jasana cave: sources disagree on which newspaper was printed there.

**Existing records not changed.** Bestansur, Qalatga Darband, Tell Shemshara and the Red Terror Museum (Amna Suraka) are approved and were left as they are.

## Not entered

- Bazyan (Wikidata 35.64 N, 44.97 E) and Gird-i Bardastee: Wikidata items with no descriptive source.
- Spy Hasar: same reason.
- The aqueducts and defiles listed on Wikidata without names.
- The old bazaar of Sulaymaniyah: no source describes it as a heritage site.
