# Kirkuk governorate discovery — 3 November 2026

This batch adds 27 Kirkuk records, which brings Kirkuk from 5 records to 32. Six of them have map pins.

- Every new record has `status: needs-review`, at least one source, and alternative names in Arabic, Turkish, Kurdish, Syriac and historical forms where sources give them.
- `build_kirkuk_records.py` defines the batch.

Kirkuk's heritage is far less documented online than Nineveh's or the Kurdistan Region's, so this list is shorter. Every record rests on an institutional, academic or official-agency source, or on a specialist inventory. Tourism sites were not used. The UNESCO Tentative List dossier for the citadel (6522) could not be re-read because of a bot challenge. Its component names come from the C1-01 capture of 2 November 2026.

## Review flags

- **Duplicate avoided.** The Wikipedia "Blue Dome" (Gök Kümbet, 14th century, Jalayirid) is the approved "Green Dome" record. Its Turkish and Arabic names could be added to that record during review.
- **Approved records not changed:** the citadel, Prophet Daniel Mosque, Mother of Sorrows Church, Green Dome and Qaysariyah.
- **Ancient name of the city.** Kirkuk Citadel is the probable site of ancient Arrapha, which lies unexcavated under the city. This is noted here instead of as a separate record.
- **Citadel quarters and gates.** These records are minimal. About 800 houses were bulldozed in 1997–98, so what survives needs field confirmation.
- **Unconfirmed details:**
  - Hasan Pakiz Mosque: its location in the citadel is disputed.
  - Sheikh Ismail shrine: its destruction is unverified.
  - Daquq bridge: its completion year (about 1883) is worked out from "142 years" in a December 2025 report.
- **Coordinates come from Wikidata and Mesopotamia Heritage.** The Wikidata point for the Sacred Heart Cathedral (44.78 E) is clearly wrong, and the cathedral (1997–2001) is modern, so it is not entered.
- **Not in Kirkuk:** Jarmo and Karim Shahir are in the Sulaymaniyah batch (Chamchamal). Tuz Khurmatu is in Salah al-Din.

## Not entered

- Other old houses in the Imam Qasim, Shorja and Musalla quarters: no named inventory is public. The directorate registers buildings over 150 years old.
